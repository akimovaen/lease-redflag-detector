Search the landlord's corpus: its lease policy, the library of approved clauses, laws, commercial committee decisions, market research and signed leases. Returns the most relevant fragments with their document, section, status (current or outdated) and date.

Use it for any question that depends on the landlord's documents or on the law. Do not use it for a specific lease of the registry (use find_leases and get_lease) or for general questions you can answer without the landlord's documents.

Write the query in Ukrainian, in the words the documents would use. Narrow it with `role` when you know the kind of source. Set `versions` to "all" only to compare with outdated versions. An empty list means nothing relevant was found; try other wording before concluding the sources say nothing.
